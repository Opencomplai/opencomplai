## What EU AI Act {{article}} covers

A distributor must verify certain things before making a high-risk system available
on the market. This is a plain summary; the Regulation text governs.

## Suggested checks

- [ ] The system carries the required marking and is accompanied by its documentation
      and instructions for use.
- [ ] The provider and the importer have met their own labelling and documentation duties.
- [ ] Storage and transport conditions do not put compliance with the requirements at risk.
- [ ] Record who ran these checks, on which version, and when.

## If in doubt

Do not make the system available until it conforms. Inform the provider or importer, and
the market surveillance authority where the system presents a risk. Keep the record of
what you decided and why.
