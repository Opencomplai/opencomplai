## What EU AI Act {{article}} covers

Before a high-risk system is placed on the market the provider follows a conformity
assessment route, either internal control or one involving a notified body, depending on
the system. This is a plain summary; the Regulation text governs.

## Suggested record

1. Decision: which route applies to this system. Write the reasoning and the Annex used.
   Your counsel confirms the route; this template does not decide it.
2. Evidence to assemble: technical documentation (Art. 11), quality management system
   (Art. 17), risk management records (Art. 9), test and evaluation results.
3. Outcome: assessment date, assessor (internal or notified body) and result.
4. Re-assessment: repeat after a substantial modification (see Art. 25) and record why.
