## What EU AI Act {{article}} covers

Article 16 collects the duties of a provider of a high-risk system. Most are owned by
a later article, so this checklist points at it. This is a plain summary; the
Regulation text governs.

## Suggested checklist

- [ ] Requirements for high-risk systems are met (Art. 8 to Art. 15).
- [ ] A quality management system is in place (Art. 17).
- [ ] Technical documentation is kept (Art. 11, Art. 18).
- [ ] Automatically generated logs are kept (Art. 12, Art. 19).
- [ ] The conformity assessment is done before market placement (Art. 43).
- [ ] The EU declaration of conformity and CE marking are prepared (Art. 47, Art. 48).
- [ ] Registration is done where required (Art. 49).
- [ ] Corrective action and information duties are assigned (Art. 20).
- [ ] The provider name and contact are shown on the system or its documentation.

Record an owner and a date next to each item you tick.
