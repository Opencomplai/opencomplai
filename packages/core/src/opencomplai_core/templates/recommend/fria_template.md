## What EU AI Act {{article}} requires

Article 27 requires certain deployers of high-risk AI systems (bodies governed
by public law, private entities providing public services, and deployers of
the credit-scoring / life-and-health-insurance-pricing systems in Annex III
points 5(b) and (c)) to carry out a fundamental rights impact assessment
(FRIA) before putting the system into use. The assessment must be documented,
and — per Art. 27(1) — must cover at minimum the elements below.

## Notes

- Art. 27(2): if any of the obligations above are already satisfied by a data
  protection impact assessment under GDPR Art. 35 or NIS2 Art. 27, this FRIA
  may complement rather than duplicate that assessment.
- Art. 27(3): the deployer must notify the relevant market surveillance
  authority of the assessment's outcome (a template questionnaire is to be
  issued by the AI Office per Art. 27(5) — check for the current version
  before submitting).
- Re-run the assessment whenever any of the factors in (a)-(f) materially
  change.
