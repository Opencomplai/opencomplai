## What EU AI Act {{article}} requires

High-risk AI systems must come with instructions for use that give deployers concise,
complete, correct and clear information (Art. 13). This statement and the 13 points
below are an unchecked reading of Art. 13(3) and are flagged `needs_founder_review`.
