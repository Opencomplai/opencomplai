# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI management system

This page covers `ISO_IEC_42001`, a pack of 65 requirements: the management-system clauses 4 to 10 and the Annex A controls of ISO/IEC 42001:2023. The standard is voluntary and has no legal application dates. Nothing here is an audit, a certification or a legal assessment.

## What it is

A native pack. Each row has the clause or control id as printed, a short heading and a one-line note in this project's own words, starting `Evidence that`. The standard's text is not reproduced; buy the standard for it. Links to the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 27001 and NIST AI RMF are the project's own judgement at low confidence.

## Does it apply to you

The pack asks four questions in the manifest under `framework_inputs.ISO_IEC_42001`:

| Question | An answer of no reads |
|---|---|
| Does the organisation develop, provide or use AI systems? | not applicable |
| Does a certification plan, customer contract or internal policy commit you to this framework? | no commitment (a yes reads applies) |
| Does the organisation develop AI systems or provide them to others? | not an AI provider |
| Does it rely on models, data or AI services from another party? | no third-party AI |

With no answers the pack reads voluntary. Confirm with your adviser.

## How rows are decided

Every row takes a provider attestation. 39 of 65 requirements checked from evidence as well: files and conventions in the repository corroborate the attestation. 26 of 65 are attestation only. The target is 28 of 65; the pack is above it. A scaffold left unfilled does not count as evidence. A row reads Met, Partial, Missing or Unverified.

## Scaffolds

Starting documents are written into the repository on request: a management-system scope statement, an AI policy, an objectives record and a statement of applicability. Templates cover data records, lifecycle evidence and management-system records, and an impact assessment section. The impact assessment scaffold is shared with the other packs of the `ai_lifecycle` family. They have `_fill in_` placeholders and are drafts, not legal advice.
